Delete your account

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your GurukulAI account (the GurukulAI Android app and app.gurukulai.in, published by Sagar Kottagattu) and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the GurukulAI app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile photo to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the App section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete Permanently.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email support@gurukulai.in from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account cancels any subscription bought through Razorpay. If you subscribed through Google Play, also cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, otherwise Google may keep charging you. Unused credits are lost when the account is deleted.

Data we delete

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, state, country and profile photo.
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase login).
  • Answer sheets and photos you uploaded, and their evaluations, scores and feedback.
  • Guru AI chat history.
  • Practice (PYQ) attempts and progress statistics.
  • Feedback you sent from the app.
  • Your plan, credit balance and notification token.

Data we keep

  • Payment records (plan, amount, date, transaction and subscription IDs). We keep these for up to 8 years as required by Indian tax and accounting laws, then delete them. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

Delete some data without deleting your account

To delete specific data, such as your chat history or uploaded answer sheets, while keeping your account, email support@gurukulai.in from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my data" and say what to delete. We will do it within 30 days.

For more about how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
