Questions

Where can I find UPSC Mains previous year questions?

GurukulAI has UPSC Mains PYQs for GS1 to GS4, searchable by year, paper and subject. Browsing them is free.

Can I get my Mains PYQ answers checked?

Yes. Write your answer, upload a photo and get section-wise marks, feedback and a model answer. Each evaluation uses 1 credit.

How should I practise Mains answer writing?

Pick one or two PYQs a day, write them in the time limit (about 7 minutes for 10 marks, 11 for 15) and get each one evaluated. Rewrite the weak ones.

Are model answers included?

Every evaluation includes a model answer you can compare yours against.
