Questions

Which NCERT books are important for UPSC?

Class 6 to 12 NCERTs for history, geography, polity and economics, plus Class 11 and 12 for art and culture, sociology and science basics. The library lists them in the order to read.

Are the NCERT PDFs free?

Yes. Every chapter links to the official free PDF on NCERT's website.

Should beginners read NCERTs before standard books?

Yes. NCERTs build the basics that standard books like Laxmikanth and Spectrum assume you already know.
